# Free & open medical / healthcare literature sources
# For Personal Groktor: discovery, methods, and wellbeing research context.
# Not medical advice. Prefer primary sources; check each item's license/ToS.
# Last updated: 2026-08-10
#
# Format: Name — URL — notes

================================================================================
PREPRINT SERVERS (open manuscripts; not always peer-reviewed)
================================================================================
bioRxiv — https://www.biorxiv.org/ — life sciences preprints; free to read
medRxiv — https://www.medrxiv.org/ — clinical / health sciences preprints; free to read
arXiv (q-bio, stat.ML health-adjacent) — https://arxiv.org/ — open preprints
OSF Preprints — https://osf.io/preprints/ — multi-discipline; includes health
Research Square — https://www.researchsquare.com/ — many free preprints (check license)
SSRN (health economics / policy subsets) — https://www.ssrn.com/ — many free full texts

================================================================================
FULL-TEXT AGGREGATORS & SEARCH (open access / free full text filters)
================================================================================
PubMed Central (PMC) — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/ — free full-text biomedical archive (NIH/NLM)
PubMed — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ — citation index; filter "Free full text" / PMC links
Europe PMC — https://europepmc.org/ — free texts + preprints + grants; API available
Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ) — https://doaj.org/ — indexed OA journals
Unpaywall — https://unpaywall.org/ — finds legal OA copies; browser extension + API
OpenAlex — https://openalex.org/ — open scholarly graph; free API
Semantic Scholar — https://www.semanticscholar.org/ — free search; OA pdf links when known
CORE — https://core.ac.uk/ — open access research aggregator
OpenAIRE Explore — https://explore.openaire.eu/ — EU open research graph
BASE (Bielefeld) — https://www.base-search.net/ — academic search; OA filter
Google Scholar — https://scholar.google.com/ — broad search; OA PDFs when available (not all free)
Lens.org — https://www.lens.org/ — scholarly + patent works; free tier
Dimensions (free discovery) — https://www.dimensions.ai/ — limited free access; check current policy
Epistemonikos — https://www.epistemonikos.org/ — systematic reviews / evidence database (free search)
Trip Medical Database — https://www.tripdatabase.com/ — clinical search; free tier with filters
SciELO — https://scielo.org/ — open access journals (Latin America / Iberia / Global South focus)
African Journals Online (AJOL) — https://www.ajol.info/ — many free/OA African journals
J-STAGE — https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/ — Japanese platform; many free articles
HAL (France) — https://hal.science/ — open archive; includes life sciences
Zenodo — https://zenodo.org/ — open research data & papers (CERN/OpenAIRE)

================================================================================

WHO IRIS — https://iris.who.int/ — WHO publications & reports (free)

NICE guidance (UK) — https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance — free clinical/public health guidance

ClinicalTrials.gov — https://clinicaltrials.gov/ — trial registry; free protocols/results when posted
ICTRP (WHO trial search) — https://www.who.int/clinical-trials-registry-platform — meta-registry search
OpenTrials / related open trials efforts — https://opentrials.net/ — open trial data projects (check status)
PROSPERO — https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/ — systematic review protocols registry
OSF Registries — https://osf.io/registries — preregistrations including health
